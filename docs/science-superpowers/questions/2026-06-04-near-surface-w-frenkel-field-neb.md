# Near-Surface W Frenkel-Pair Field NEB

**Research question:** For a near-surface tungsten Frenkel-pair hop or recombination pathway, does an explicit-field ASE calculator wrapper applied to CHGNet qualitatively lower the NEB migration barrier relative to the no-field CHGNet control, and is any observed trend strong enough to justify BigDFT validation?

**Background / motivation:** The flash-sintering workflow needs a small, testable bridge between qualitative field-driven intuition and the existing DFT-gated electrodefect program. Near-surface Frenkel-pair motion is closer to the work-function and emission story than a purely bulk hop, while remaining small enough for a controlled NEB screen. CHGNet is used here only as a fast exploratory potential; accepted claims still require DFT validation.

**Hypotheses:**
- H0 (null): The explicit-field wrapper does not produce a reproducible qualitative reduction in the CHGNet NEB barrier for the selected near-surface W Frenkel-pair pathway relative to the matched no-field control, or the pathway becomes unstable/unphysical under the wrapper.
- H1 (alternative): The explicit-field wrapper produces a reproducible qualitative reduction in the CHGNet NEB barrier for the selected pathway, with geometries and endpoint energetics that remain plausible enough to promote selected images to BigDFT validation.

**Population & unit of analysis:** The population is near-surface W Frenkel-pair configurations generated by the existing `flash-modelling` structure builders and Phase-2 staging scripts. The unit of analysis is one defined NEB pathway: fixed initial structure, fixed final structure, fixed image count, fixed optimizer settings, and one specified field/charge assignment.

**Key variables (operationalized):**
- Outcome: CHGNet NEB barrier change -> field-wrapper barrier minus matched no-field barrier for the same initial/final pathway and NEB settings.
- Predictor / exposure: field condition -> no-field CHGNet calculator versus CHGNet wrapped by an ASE electric-field calculator with documented `field_strength`, `direction`, and explicit per-atom charges.
- Required control: the no-field CHGNet NEB run on the identical pathway.
- Promotion variables: endpoint energy difference, image energy ordering, maximum force convergence status, final image geometries, and whether the field-run path remains chemically/geometrically plausible.
- Validation target: selected initial, saddle-like, and final images promoted to BigDFT if the CHGNet trend is coherent and not an artifact of the charge assignment.

**What counts as an answer:** A useful exploratory answer exists if the no-field and field-wrapper NEB runs complete for the same near-surface W pathway and produce finite barriers with inspectable image geometries. A positive exploratory answer is a coherent field-associated barrier reduction that survives basic geometry and endpoint sanity checks. A negative answer is no reduction, unstable/unphysical images, or sensitivity so strong that the result is dominated by the arbitrary charge model.

**Scope & exclusions:** This question does not claim real electric-field physics from CHGNet, does not model hot-electron emission or electron-hole recombination, does not treat CHGNet magnetic moments as charges, and does not determine which morphology flash physically forms. It is a screening question for whether a small field-perturbed pathway is worth DFT validation.

**Open questions for prior-work survey:** The next methods check should identify appropriate field-aware MLIP references, whether fixed-charge field wrappers have any defensible use as qualitative perturbations for metallic defect pathways, and what DFT setup is most appropriate for validating a near-surface W Frenkel-pair path under or without an external field.
